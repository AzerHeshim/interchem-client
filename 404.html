Interchem is a supplier of agricultural inputs and technologies focused on fertilizers, crop protection products and agronomic solutions. The company works with farmers and agribusinesses to support modern and efficient agricultural production.

Who is Interchem?

Supporting modern agriculture with reliable inputs and expertise

Interchem is a supplier of agricultural inputs, agrochemical products and farming technologies. Our goal is to help farmers increase productivity and support the development of modern agriculture.

Our mission

To provide farmers and agribusinesses with high-quality agricultural products and effective agronomic solutions.

Our vision

To become a reliable and innovative supplier in the agricultural sector of Azerbaijan.
