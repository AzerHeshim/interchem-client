Who is Interchem?

Interchem specializes in the supply of environmentally friendly and efficient fertilizers and crop protection products, the design of intensive farming systems, and the provision of comprehensive agrotechnological support, including agronomic advisory and agribusiness management services.

Our mission

Using modern technologies, we provide farmers with integrated solutions, including high-efficiency, environmentally friendly plant nutrition and crop protection products to support the sustainable development of farming operations.

Our vision

Our vision is to become a market leader by driving the development and adoption of advanced, efficient agricultural technologies that enable sustainable farming without harming the environment.

Our Services

We do not simply sell agrochemical products and farming systems. Instead, we begin by identifying the real needs and challenges of each farming operation, analyzing climate and soil conditions, and taking budget considerations into account. Based on this, we deliver tailored agrotechnological solutions that ensure the efficient use of resources, supported by ongoing technical advisory services.
